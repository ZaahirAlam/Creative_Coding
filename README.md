# Dear Data 100
The Scroll Cycle
Are smartphones a modern necessity or have they transitioned into a digital addiction. 

Over the last few weeks, I recorded instances when I used an app on my phone. This helped me explore whether my phone was a daily necessity or just an addiction. The data was collected on paper, in which I recorded what apps I used and how long I used them for, and then ultimately transferred into csv format.

Each row tracks:
- Date and Month: When I used the app
- App Names: Specific apps like Monzo, Tiktok, or Moodle
- Category: Grouped into Entertainment, Education, Communication, Food, Health, or Banking.
- Duration: How long I used each app for (i.e <5 mins, 10 mins, or 30 mins etc).

Visual encoding key for my sketch:

Timeline (X-Axis): Chronological progression from Left to Right/
Vertical Stacking: When multiple apps are used on the same day, they stack directly going down.

Rules for the shapes and colours:
- App Icon: A small image of the app logo goes in the exact middle
- Colour of Rings: Rings go around the app icon based on the category.
- Red is for Entertainment (Snapchat, Tiktok, Youtube)
- Blue is for Education (Outlook, Seats, Moodle)
- Green is for Health (Puregym)
- Yellow is for Food (Deliveroo, Just Eat)
- Orange is for Communication (Whatsapp)
- Purple is for Banking (Monzo)

Number of rings:
- The number of rings shows how long I used the app. Quick checks have fewer rings, and long checks have the most rings.
- Snapchat Shift: Snapchat changes colours between Red (Entertainment) and Orange (communication) depending on how I used it during that specific look.
